How It Works

From idea to bedtime story in 60 seconds

Four simple steps — then sit back and listen to the magic unfold.

Features

Everything a great story needs

Story Magic is packed with magic — and it all runs right in your browser.

⚡

Instant AI Stories

Powered by Groq's Llama 3.3, stories generate in seconds — personalised, original, and always age-appropriate.

🎙️

Read-Aloud Narration

Listen along with device voices, or upgrade to ultra-realistic ElevenLabs or OpenAI TTS with a single tap.

🎨

AI Illustrations

Tap to copy your story prompt and open Gemini, then save the artwork into your storybook. Have a paid Gemini key? Generate art for every page right in the app.

📚

Personal Library

Every story is saved with artwork. Re-read favourites, export as PDF, or swap the illustration anytime.

🏆

XP & Achievements

Kids earn XP and level up from Explorer to Legend as they read and create — keeping the magic alive.

📄

Export as PDF

Turn any story into a PDF book with its artwork, ready to print at home or take to a print shop.

👨‍👩‍👧

Parent Dashboard

Set child profiles, manage API keys, export story collections, and review all activity in a secure parent area.

🔒

Privacy First

Stories, heroes and keys stay on your device. No accounts and no cloud library. Story and narration text is sent only to the services you switch on with your own keys.

📲

Install as an App

Add Story Magic to your home screen for a full app experience on iOS and Android — no app store needed.
